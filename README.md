# BA-HUB

Business Analysis Hub (BA-HUB) is a curated collection of business analysis prompts, templates, and reusable artifacts designed to support analysts, product managers, project stakeholders, and consultants.

This workspace brings together two main libraries:

- BA Prompts: structured prompts for business analysis tasks, frameworks, and deliverables
- BA Templates: editable business templates for documents, spreadsheets, and presentations

---

## Purpose

BA-HUB helps teams accelerate work across the full business analysis lifecycle, including:

- stakeholder analysis
- requirements gathering and documentation
- process analysis and modeling
- solution evaluation
- Agile and product discovery work
- testing, validation, and UAT
- reporting, dashboards, and governance
- strategy, product, and portfolio planning

## What is included

### BA Prompts
The prompt library covers a broad set of business analysis topics and is intended for use with AI-assisted workflows, consulting engagements, and internal documentation tasks. It includes specialized prompt packs for areas such as:

- fundamentals and business analysis concepts
- requirements analysis and validation
- stakeholder and communication management
- process and BPMN modeling
- UML and UX wireframing
- SQL, API, and integration analysis
- Agile, Scrum, and user stories
- product strategy, analytics, and growth
- AI usage in business analysis
- reporting, UAT, and real-world system analysis

### BA Templates
The template library contains reusable files for business analysis work products, including:

- business requirements documents
- stakeholder maps and matrices
- process analysis and modeling artifacts
- solution assessment reports
- business cases and recommendation documents
- governance, compliance, and benefits tracking
- KPI dashboards and reporting packs
- UAT and validation trackers

The templates are provided in common business formats such as:

- DOCX for reports and plans
- XLSX for registers, trackers, and matrices
- PPTX for presentations and workshops

## How to use this repository

1. Browse the topic folders in BA Prompts to find a prompt pack relevant to your scenario.
2. Open the matching category in BA Templates to access reusable document or spreadsheet structures.
3. Customize placeholders, company names, logos, scoring values, and workflow details for your context.
4. Use the files as starting points for client work, internal initiatives, backlog planning, or stakeholder communications.
